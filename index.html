About Us

Who We Are

Girl Scout Troop 4210 is a volunteer-led troop based in Sunnybrook, proudly chartered under the Sunnybrook Service Unit. We bring together girls from Daisies (grade K) all the way through Ambassadors (grade 12) for weekly meetings, hands-on badge work, community service, and unforgettable outdoor adventures.

Our mission is simple: give every girl in our troop the chance to discover who she is, connect with new friends, and take action to make her community better — all while having fun. We follow the Girl Scout Law and Promise in everything we do, and we believe every girl deserves a safe, encouraging space to try new things.

Our Mission

To build girls of courage, confidence, and character who make the world a better place.

When We Meet

Tuesdays, 6:00–7:30 PM during the school year at Sunnybrook Community Center, Room 12.

Who Can Join

Girls in grades K–12 who live in or attend school in the greater Sunnybrook area.
