About Us

Who We Are

Girl Scout Troop 2026 is a volunteer-led troop based in Sunnybrook, proudly chartered under the Sunnybrook Service Unit. We bring together girls from Daisies (grade K) all the way through Ambassadors (grade 12) for weekly meetings, hands-on badge work, community service, and unforgettable outdoor adventures.

Our mission is simple: give every girl in our troop the chance to discover who she is, connect with new friends, and take action to make her community better — all while having fun. We follow the Girl Scout Law and Promise in everything we do, and we believe every girl deserves a safe, encouraging space to try new things.

Our Mission

To build girls of courage, confidence, and character who make the world a better place.

When We Meet

Tuesdays, 6:00–7:30 PM during the school year at Sunnybrook Community Center, Room 12.

Who Can Join

Girls in grades K–12 who live in or attend school in the greater Sunnybrook area.

Support Our Troop

Make a Donation

Troop 2026 is entirely funded by cookie and fall product sales, along with the generosity of our community. Your donation helps cover badge supplies, campout fees, and financial assistance so every girl can participate, regardless of cost.

Monetary Donation

One-time or recurring gifts go directly toward badges, event fees, and financial assistance for girls in our troop.

Wish List Items

Craft supplies, camping gear, and first-aid kits are always needed. Contact us for our current wish list.

Corporate Matching

Many employers match charitable gifts. Ask your HR department if your donation can be doubled.
